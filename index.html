How to explore

  • Drag anywhere to orbit the camera around the cafe.
  • W A S D or the arrow keys turn the view; Q / E zoom in and out.
  • Scroll to dolly. The camera is fully interactive in three dimensions.
  • Click the red valve on the espresso machine to release steam.
  • Night mode dims the room and lights the warm cafe lamps.
  • Sound is strictly off until you explicitly press the button; no audio ever autoplays.
  • Export downloads the whole procedural diorama as an OBJ or STL mesh.
  • Scene diagnostics reports exact surface area, enclosed volume, gear ratios, foam statistics and a stable field signature. Every value comes from the pure geometry engine.
  • P pauses motion, R resets the camera, and the scene seed input rebuilds the whole crystal garden deterministically.
  • H toggles this card, Esc closes it, and the Keyboard shortcuts switch in the panel turns every single-key control off.
